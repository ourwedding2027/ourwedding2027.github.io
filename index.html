Your journey to Ireland

Whether you’re travelling from near or far, we’re so grateful you’re making the journey to celebrate with us.

We’ve gathered the essentials to help you plan your visit: airport options, getting around and travelling to the wedding venues, accommodation, and a few ideas for exploring Ireland while you’re here.

There are several ways to make your way to the wedding, and the best option really depends on how long you are staying and what you would like to make of your time in Ireland and Northern Ireland.

Getting Here

♥
BFS

Belfast International Airport

Belfast International is the closest major airport to the wedding area and a good option if you would prefer to arrive directly into Northern Ireland.

Direct flights from Budapest have operated seasonally in previous years, so it is worth checking Ryanair and easyJet first. If no direct route is available, common connections include:

  • Budapest or Debrecen → London Luton → Belfast — Wizz Air + easyJet
  • Budapest → London Stansted → Belfast — Ryanair

From the airport, you can continue to Belfast or Cookstown by bus, taxi or hire car.

Best for: Northern Ireland · Causeway Coast · Shorter journey to Cookstown

Approx. 40 minutes from the venue · Car & bus options available
BHD

Belfast City Airport

Belfast City Airport is very close to the city centre and a great option if you would like to spend some time exploring Belfast before travelling to Cookstown.

Flights from Hungary require a connection. Common options include:

  • Budapest → Amsterdam → Belfast City — KLM
  • Budapest → London Heathrow → Belfast City — British Airways
  • Budapest → Birmingham (BHX) → Belfast City — Ryanair + Aer Lingus

If you stay centrally, many of Belfast’s main sights are easy to reach on foot or by public transport.

From Belfast, regular bus services continue to Cookstown.

Best for: Belfast city break · Easy city-centre access · Northern Ireland sightseeing

Approx. 1 hour from the venue · Car & bus options available
DUB

Dublin Airport

Dublin is one of the easiest options for guests travelling from Hungary, with direct flights from Budapest currently available with Aer Lingus and Ryanair.

It is also a great choice if you would like to spend some time exploring Dublin or visit the west coast, including Galway, Doolin and the Cliffs of Moher.

From Dublin, you can continue north by coach, train to Belfast, or hire a car. Direct coach options to Cookstown are also available.

Best for: Direct flights from Budapest · Dublin sightseeing · West Coast trips · Flexible onward travel

Approx. 2 hours from the venue · Car & coach options available

Remember Flight schedules and routes may change before October 2027, so these are intended as examples only. Please check current schedules and connection times before booking. Getting Around

♥

There are several ways to get to and around the area. Choose the option that works best for you.

Car Hire

A car can be a great option if you would like flexibility, especially for rural areas or the North Coast.

Cars drive on the left in both the Republic of Ireland and Northern Ireland, and a valid EU driving licence is accepted for visitors. If hiring in Dublin, check that cross-border travel into Northern Ireland is permitted and covered.

The Republic of Ireland uses kilometres and km/h, while Northern Ireland uses miles and mph.

Coaches

Direct coach services are available from Dublin Airport to Cookstown with Translink X4 and AirCoach.

For Dublin to Belfast, there are frequent services with Aircoach, Dublin Express and Translink X1/X2.

From Belfast to Cookstown, Translink operates regular 209/209b services from Belfast Grand Central Station.

Plan Your Journey with Translink | Bus & Train NI AirCoach Dublin Express

Trains

Trains run directly between Dublin Connolly and Belfast Grand Central Station, making it an easy and comfortable city-centre-to-city-centre option.

There is no direct rail connection from Belfast to Cookstown, so the onward journey needs to be made by coach, taxi or car.

Plan Your Journey with Translink | Bus & Train NI

Day Tours

You don’t need a car to explore more of Ireland. Day tours run from Belfast to the North Coast, including Dunluce Castle, Portrush and Game of Thrones locations, and from Dublin to Galway, Doolin and the Cliffs of Moher.

North Coast tours from Belfast West Coast Tours from Dublin Galway Tours from Dublin

Quick Facts

♥

Driving

Drive on the left

Currency

Republic of Ireland — € EUR

Northern Ireland — £ GBP

Entry

Hungarian/EU citizens: travel with a valid passport, not a national ID card. Please check UK ETA requirements before travelling to Northern Ireland

Plugs

Type G three-pin plugs. Bring an adapter from home, or get one at the airport before you travel.

Mobile

EU roaming applies in Ireland; check UK roaming with your provider

Emergency

112 or 999

Weather

October is usually around 10–15°C and can feel quite mild, but the weather is changeable. Bring layers, a waterproof jacket and an umbrella.

Border Crossing

No border checks between the Republic of Ireland and Northern Ireland, but UK entry and ETA requirements still apply.

Payment

Cards and contactless are widely accepted.

Travel requirements can change, so please check the latest official guidance before travelling.
